Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

These apps are used only by their owner to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page. They are not offered to other users.

Data collected

The apps do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data from other people. The only access used is the owner's own access to their YouTube channel and Facebook Page, which is used only to upload and publish content.

Data deletion

No user data is stored. If you believe any of your data was processed by these apps and want it deleted, contact the owner through the GitHub profile at github.com/mynrd and it will be removed.
